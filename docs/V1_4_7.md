# AI-KIT v1.4.7

Installer options become machine-readable and checked: `aikit flags` prints the `aikit install` option schema, [installer-flags.json](../integrations/installer-flags.json) is its committed copy, and kit validation fails when the schema, the [flag guide](guide/INSTALLER_FLAGS.md) or SKILL.md drifts from the parser. Gemini routing declares its documented per-model thinking levels. [CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md), [ADR 0017](adr/0017-installer-flag-schema-and-gemini-thinking.md).

Installation behavior is unchanged: options, defaults, presets and installed project files stay as in v1.4.6, apart from the updated router guidance (`router/POLICY.md`, `router/providers/gemini.md`, `gemini.json`), which upgrades through the normal preserved-conflict path.
